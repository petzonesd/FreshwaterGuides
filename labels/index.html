Care Label Printer

By Pet Zone SD TeamUpdated October 7, 2026works in your browser, nothing is uploaded

Paste your stock list, confirm the matches, and print tank labels with a QR code that opens the matching care guide. Four pieces on US Letter paper: a small label for the tag holder, a large label with the full line of facts, a half-page care sheet that goes home in the bag, and a counter sign. Your own names and prices go on them.

1. Where is it going?

2. Paste your stock list

One fish, shrimp or plant per line. Add a price after a | or a $ if you want it on the labels, for example Neon Tetra | $2.99. Common alternate names work (CPD, SAE, bristlenose).

3. Check the matches

4. Choose the pieces

In the print dialog choose paper size Letter, scale 100% (not “fit to page”), and turn headers and footers off. If the care sheets print upright, switch the orientation to Landscape.
