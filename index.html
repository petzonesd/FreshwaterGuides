Why Freshwater Guides

Built for keepers who want the real answer.

Freshwater Guides is an educational resource for people keeping freshwater fish, shrimp, plants, and aquascapes. We focus on clear explanations, sensible husbandry, and advice that holds up in a real living-room tank.

Our work is informed by hands-on aquarium retail and keeping experience. When an affiliated aquarium business is directly relevant to a guide, we may link to it transparently. A good answer should still stand on its own.
