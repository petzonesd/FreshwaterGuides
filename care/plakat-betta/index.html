Plakat Betta Care Guide

By Pet Zone SD TeamUpdated October 7, 20262 min read

Plakat Betta (Betta splendens (short-finned)) is a beginner-level freshwater fish that needs at least 5 gallons and 76-82°F. Plakat bettas are short-finned varieties, active and hardy. Keep males singly, and pair only with calm tankmates.

Care facts at a glance

Scientific nameBetta splendens (short-finned)
FamilyOsphronemidae
OriginCaptive-bred, Southeast Asia origin
Adult size2.5 in
Minimum tank5 gallons
Temperature76-82°F
pH6.0-7.5
TemperamentAggressive toward other bettas
Social behaviorSolitary
Swim levelUpper
DifficultyBeginner
DietCarnivore leaning omnivore: betta pellets, frozen foods
Typical lifespan3-5 years

Quick tips

  • One male per tank.
  • Heated, filtered tanks only.

San Diego water

Captive-bred stock usually adapts to San Diego tap water with slow acclimation. For best color and health, many keepers blend tap with RO/DI water. Learn the numbers in our San Diego tap water guide.

Before you buy

Make sure your tank is cycled (how to cycle a tank), check your numbers with the water testing guide, and use the compatibility guide to check tankmates. These are general ranges; individual fish and local water vary.
