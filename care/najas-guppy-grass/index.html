Guppy Grass Care Guide

By Pet Zone SD Team · Updated October 7, 2026 · 2 min read

Guppy Grass (Najas guadalupensis) is a beginner-level aquarium plant. A delicate, fast-growing plant that hides fry and absorbs nutrients.

Care facts at a glance

Scientific nameNajas guadalupensis
FamilyHydrocharitaceae
OriginAmericas
Height12-24 in
LightLow to medium
CO2Not needed
PlacementFloating or anchored, stem
Growth rateFast
Temperature65-86°F
pH6.0-8.0
DifficultyBeginner
PropagationCuttings

Quick tips

  • Trim often.
  • Great for fry cover.

San Diego water

Tolerates San Diego's hard, alkaline tap water (pH roughly 7.6-8.6) with slow acclimation and a conditioner that handles chloramine. Learn the numbers in our San Diego tap water guide.
