Paradise Fish Care Guide

By Pet Zone SD TeamUpdated October 7, 20262 min read

Paradise Fish (Macropodus opercularis) is a intermediate-level freshwater fish that needs at least 20 gallons and 60-78°F. A hardy, colorful labyrinth fish that tolerates cool water but is aggressive toward its own kind and small tankmates.

Care facts at a glance

Scientific nameMacropodus opercularis
FamilyOsphronemidae
OriginEast Asia
Adult size4 in
Minimum tank20 gallons
Temperature60-78°F
pH6.0-8.0
TemperamentAggressive
Social behaviorSolitary or pairs
Swim levelUpper
DifficultyIntermediate
DietOmnivore: flakes, pellets, frozen foods
Typical lifespan6-8 years

Quick tips

  • Keep one male per tank.
  • Use a lid with a gap for air.

San Diego water

Tolerates San Diego's hard, alkaline tap water (pH roughly 7.6-8.6) with slow acclimation and a conditioner that handles chloramine. Learn the numbers in our San Diego tap water guide.

Before you buy

Make sure your tank is cycled (how to cycle a tank), check your numbers with the water testing guide, and use the compatibility guide to check tankmates. These are general ranges; individual fish and local water vary.
