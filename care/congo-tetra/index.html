Congo Tetra Care Guide

By Pet Zone SD Team · Updated October 7, 2026 · 2 min read

Congo Tetra (Phenacogrammus interruptus) is a intermediate-level freshwater fish that needs at least 29 gallons and 73-82°F. A larger, shimmering tetra whose fins lengthen as males mature. Needs open swimming room and a gentle current.

Care facts at a glance

Scientific namePhenacogrammus interruptus
FamilyAlestidae
OriginCongo basin, Africa
Adult size3.5 in
Minimum tank29 gallons
Temperature73-82°F
pH6.0-7.5
TemperamentPeaceful
Social behaviorSchooling, keep 6+
Swim levelMid
DifficultyIntermediate
DietOmnivore: flakes, pellets, frozen foods
Typical lifespan3-5 years

Quick tips

  • Needs a longer tank for swimming.
  • Slow, long-finned fish may get harassed.
  • Keep a group of six or more.

San Diego water

Captive-bred stock usually adapts to San Diego tap water with slow acclimation. For best color and health, many keepers blend tap with RO/DI water. Learn the numbers in our San Diego tap water guide.

Before you buy

Make sure your tank is cycled (how to cycle a tank), check your numbers with the water testing guide, and use the compatibility guide to check tankmates. These are general ranges; individual fish and local water vary.
