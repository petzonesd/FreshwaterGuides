Electric Blue Jack Dempsey Care Guide

By Pet Zone SD Team · Updated October 7, 2026 · 2 min read

Electric Blue Jack Dempsey (Rocio octofasciata (blue variety)) is a intermediate-level freshwater fish that needs at least 75 gallons and 75-82°F. A vivid blue cultivated form of the Jack Dempsey. Territorial and not suited to small or peaceful tankmates.

Care facts at a glance

Scientific nameRocio octofasciata (blue variety)
FamilyCichlidae
OriginCaptive-bred
Adult size10 in
Minimum tank75 gallons
Temperature75-82°F
pH6.5-8.0
TemperamentAggressive
Social behaviorSolitary or pair
Swim levelMid
DifficultyIntermediate
DietCarnivore leaning omnivore: pellets, frozen foods
Typical lifespan10-15 years

Quick tips

  • Needs a large tank.
  • Keep with similarly sized fish.

San Diego water

Tolerates San Diego's hard, alkaline tap water (pH roughly 7.6-8.6) with slow acclimation and a conditioner that handles chloramine. Learn the numbers in our San Diego tap water guide.

Before you buy

Make sure your tank is cycled (how to cycle a tank), check your numbers with the water testing guide, and use the compatibility guide to check tankmates. These are general ranges; individual fish and local water vary.
