Comet Goldfish Care Guide

By Pet Zone SD Team · Updated October 7, 2026 · 2 min read

Comet Goldfish (Carassius auratus) is a beginner-level freshwater fish that needs at least 75 gallons and 60-75°F. The classic long-bodied, single-tailed goldfish. Active and large, and best suited to a pond or a very large tank.

Care facts at a glance

Scientific nameCarassius auratus
FamilyCyprinidae
OriginCultivated in East Asia
Adult size10-12 in
Minimum tank75 gallons
Temperature60-75°F
pH6.5-8.0
TemperamentPeaceful, very active
Social behaviorGroups
Swim levelAll levels
DifficultyBeginner
DietOmnivore: goldfish pellets, veggies
Typical lifespan10-20 years

Quick tips

  • Needs a pond or very large tank.
  • Heavy waste: strong filtration.
  • Do not keep with tropical fish.

San Diego water

Tolerates San Diego's hard, alkaline tap water (pH roughly 7.6-8.6) with slow acclimation and a conditioner that handles chloramine. Learn the numbers in our San Diego tap water guide.

Before you buy

Make sure your tank is cycled (how to cycle a tank), check your numbers with the water testing guide, and use the compatibility guide to check tankmates. These are general ranges; individual fish and local water vary.
