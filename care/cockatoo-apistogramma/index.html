Cockatoo Apistogramma Care Guide

By Pet Zone SD Team · Updated October 7, 2026 · 2 min read

Cockatoo Apistogramma (Apistogramma cacatuoides) is a intermediate-level freshwater fish that needs at least 20 gallons and 73-82°F. A colorful, easier dwarf cichlid. Males set up territories and need caves and line-of-sight breaks. See our dedicated Apistogramma guide at apistogrammarama.com.

Care facts at a glance

Scientific nameApistogramma cacatuoides
FamilyCichlidae
OriginPeru and Brazil
Adult size2-3.5 in
Minimum tank20 gallons
Temperature73-82°F
pH6.0-7.5
TemperamentPeaceful for a cichlid; territorial
Social behaviorOne male with 2-3 females
Swim levelBottom to mid
DifficultyIntermediate
DietMicro pellets, frozen and live foods
Typical lifespan3-5 years

Quick tips

  • Provide a cave per female.
  • Keep one male per tank unless it is large.
  • Use leaf litter, caves, plants.

San Diego water

Captive-bred stock usually adapts to San Diego tap water with slow acclimation. For best color and health, many keepers blend tap with RO/DI water. Learn the numbers in our San Diego tap water guide.

Before you buy

Make sure your tank is cycled (how to cycle a tank), check your numbers with the water testing guide, and use the compatibility guide to check tankmates. These are general ranges; individual fish and local water vary.
