Staurogyne Repens Care Guide

By Pet Zone SD Team · Updated October 7, 2026 · 2 min read

Staurogyne Repens (Staurogyne repens) is a beginner-level aquarium plant. A low, bushy plant that forms a lush carpet and is easier than many foreground plants.

Care facts at a glance

Scientific nameStaurogyne repens
FamilyAcanthaceae
OriginSouth America
Height3 in
LightMedium
CO2Optional
PlacementForeground to midground, carpeting stem
Growth rateModerate
Temperature70-82°F
pH6.0-7.5
DifficultyBeginner
PropagationCuttings

Quick tips

  • Trim to keep it low.
  • Moderate light is enough.

San Diego water

Captive-bred stock usually adapts to San Diego tap water with slow acclimation. For best color and health, many keepers blend tap with RO/DI water. Learn the numbers in our San Diego tap water guide.
