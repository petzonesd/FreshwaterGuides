Giant Danio Care Guide

By Pet Zone SD Team · Updated October 7, 2026 · 2 min read

Giant Danio (Devario aequipinnatus) is a beginner-level freshwater fish that needs at least 30 gallons and 65-80°F. A fast, hardy danio that needs a long tank and a tight lid. Great for active community tanks with similarly sized fish.

Care facts at a glance

Scientific nameDevario aequipinnatus
FamilyDanionidae
OriginSouth Asia
Adult size4 in
Minimum tank30 gallons
Temperature65-80°F
pH6.0-8.0
TemperamentActive, peaceful
Social behaviorSchooling, keep 6+
Swim levelUpper to mid
DifficultyBeginner
DietOmnivore: flakes, pellets, frozen foods
Typical lifespan4-5 years

Quick tips

  • Use a tight lid; they jump.
  • Give it a long tank for swimming.

San Diego water

Tolerates San Diego's hard, alkaline tap water (pH roughly 7.6-8.6) with slow acclimation and a conditioner that handles chloramine. Learn the numbers in our San Diego tap water guide.

Before you buy

Make sure your tank is cycled (how to cycle a tank), check your numbers with the water testing guide, and use the compatibility guide to check tankmates. These are general ranges; individual fish and local water vary.
