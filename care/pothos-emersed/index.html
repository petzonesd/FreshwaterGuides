Pothos and Houseplants for Tanks Care Guide

By Pet Zone SD Team · Updated October 7, 2026 · 2 min read

Pothos and Houseplants for Tanks (Epipremnum and related species) is a beginner-level aquarium plant. Pothos, philodendron and other houseplants can grow roots in aquarium water to absorb nutrients. Only the roots go in the water; the leaves stay in the air.

Care facts at a glance

Scientific nameEpipremnum and related species
FamilyAraceae
OriginTropics
Heightvaries
LightLow to high
CO2Not needed
PlacementRoots in water, leaves out of water
Growth rateFast
Temperature65-86°F
pH6.0-8.0
DifficultyBeginner
PropagationCuttings

Quick tips

  • Keep leaves above water.
  • Great for nitrate control.

San Diego water

Tolerates San Diego's hard, alkaline tap water (pH roughly 7.6-8.6) with slow acclimation and a conditioner that handles chloramine. Learn the numbers in our San Diego tap water guide.
