Tiger Lotus Care Guide

By Pet Zone SD Team · Updated October 7, 2026 · 2 min read

Tiger Lotus (Nymphaea species) is a beginner-level aquarium plant. A bulb plant with big, speckled leaves that can reach the surface. Trim floating leaves to let light through.

Care facts at a glance

Scientific nameNymphaea species
FamilyNymphaeaceae
OriginTropical regions
Height8-20 in
LightMedium to high
CO2Optional
PlacementMidground to background, bulb
Growth rateFast
Temperature68-86°F
pH6.0-7.5
DifficultyBeginner
PropagationBulb division

Quick tips

  • Trim surface leaves.
  • Plant the bulb half-buried.

San Diego water

Captive-bred stock usually adapts to San Diego tap water with slow acclimation. For best color and health, many keepers blend tap with RO/DI water. Learn the numbers in our San Diego tap water guide.
