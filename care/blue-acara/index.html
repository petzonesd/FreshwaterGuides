Blue Acara Care Guide

By Pet Zone SD TeamUpdated October 7, 20262 min read

Blue Acara (Andinoacara pulcher) is a intermediate-level freshwater fish that needs at least 55 gallons and 72-82°F. A hardy, medium-sized cichlid with blue-green markings. It is calmer than most large cichlids but will eat small fish and defend a territory when breeding.

Care facts at a glance

Scientific nameAndinoacara pulcher
FamilyCichlidae
OriginPanama, Colombia and Venezuela
Adult size6-8 in
Minimum tank55 gallons
Temperature72-82°F
pH6.5-7.8
TemperamentSemi-aggressive, territorial when breeding
Social behaviorPair or solitary
Swim levelBottom to mid
DifficultyIntermediate
DietOmnivore: cichlid pellets, frozen foods, vegetables
Typical lifespan8-10 years

Quick tips

  • Do not keep with small fish like neons.
  • Provide caves and open swimming space.
  • Use a sturdy lid and strong filtration.

San Diego water

Tolerates San Diego's hard, alkaline tap water (pH roughly 7.6-8.6) with slow acclimation and a conditioner that handles chloramine. Learn the numbers in our San Diego tap water guide.

Before you buy

Make sure your tank is cycled (how to cycle a tank), check your numbers with the water testing guide, and use the compatibility guide to check tankmates. These are general ranges; individual fish and local water vary.
