Hornwort Care Guide

By Pet Zone SD TeamUpdated October 7, 20262 min read

Hornwort (Ceratophyllum demersum) is a beginner-level aquarium plant. A fast-growing, rootless plant that absorbs nutrients and shelters fry. It can shed needles when stressed.

Care facts at a glance

Scientific nameCeratophyllum demersum
FamilyCeratophyllaceae
OriginWorldwide
Height24+ in
LightLow to medium
CO2Optional
PlacementFloating or anchored, stem
Growth rateFast
Temperature60-86°F
pH6.0-8.0
DifficultyBeginner
PropagationCuttings

Quick tips

  • Float it or loosely anchor it.
  • Trim often.

San Diego water

Tolerates San Diego's hard, alkaline tap water (pH roughly 7.6-8.6) with slow acclimation and a conditioner that handles chloramine. Learn the numbers in our San Diego tap water guide.
