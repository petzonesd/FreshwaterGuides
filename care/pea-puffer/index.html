Pea Puffer Care Guide

By Pet Zone SD Team · Updated October 7, 2026 · 2 min read

Pea Puffer (Carinotetraodon travancoricus) is a intermediate-level freshwater fish that needs at least 5 gallons and 74-82°F. The smallest puffer. Personable but nippy and a picky carnivore that needs snails, frozen foods and sometimes live foods.

Care facts at a glance

Scientific nameCarinotetraodon travancoricus
FamilyTetraodontidae
OriginSouthwest India
Adult size1-1.4 in
Minimum tank5 gallons
Temperature74-82°F
pH6.8-7.8
TemperamentSemi-aggressive
Social behaviorSolitary in 5 gal; groups in 15+ gal with lots of plants
Swim levelMid
DifficultyIntermediate
DietCarnivore: live and frozen foods; snails help wear their beak
Typical lifespan4-5 years

Quick tips

  • Needs live or frozen foods; no flakes.
  • Do not keep with fin-nipping-vulnerable tankmates.
  • Add plenty of plants for line of sight breaks.

San Diego water

Tolerates San Diego's hard, alkaline tap water (pH roughly 7.6-8.6) with slow acclimation and a conditioner that handles chloramine. Learn the numbers in our San Diego tap water guide.

Before you buy

Make sure your tank is cycled (how to cycle a tank), check your numbers with the water testing guide, and use the compatibility guide to check tankmates. These are general ranges; individual fish and local water vary.
