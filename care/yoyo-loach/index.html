Yoyo Loach Care Guide

By Pet Zone SD TeamUpdated October 7, 20262 min read

Yoyo Loach (Botia almorhae) is a intermediate-level freshwater fish that needs at least 40 gallons and 73-80°F. An active, playful loach with a pattern that resembles the letters of its name when young. Best in a group.

Care facts at a glance

Scientific nameBotia almorhae
FamilyBotiidae
OriginIndia and Pakistan
Adult size6 in
Minimum tank40 gallons
Temperature73-80°F
pH6.5-7.5
TemperamentActive, semi-aggressive
Social behaviorSmall groups
Swim levelBottom
DifficultyIntermediate
DietOmnivore: pellets, frozen foods, snails
Typical lifespan10-15 years

Quick tips

  • Keep a group of three or more.
  • Provide caves.

San Diego water

Captive-bred stock usually adapts to San Diego tap water with slow acclimation. For best color and health, many keepers blend tap with RO/DI water. Learn the numbers in our San Diego tap water guide.

Before you buy

Make sure your tank is cycled (how to cycle a tank), check your numbers with the water testing guide, and use the compatibility guide to check tankmates. These are general ranges; individual fish and local water vary.
